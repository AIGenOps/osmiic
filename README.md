# OSMIIC

### The Vintage Tape Deck Media Downloader

OSMIIC brings the tactile nostalgia of classic 80s and 90s cassette decks into the modern digital era. Enjoy your favorite music and videos offline with animated spinning tape reels, glowing VU meters, studio-quality sound, and an automated library organizer that keeps your collection neat without any extra effort.

---

## Why OSMIIC?

Most media downloaders are clunky, clinical, and cluttered. OSMIIC was crafted for music lovers who appreciate the warm aesthetic of analog sound systems and the convenience of modern technology.

- **Feel the Music**: Watch tape reels spin and dynamic VU meters react as your tracks arrive on your machine.
- **Always Organized**: Say goodbye to a chaotic Downloads folder. OSMIIC automatically identifies the language and genre of every track and files them into dedicated music and video folders.
- **Audiophile Fidelity**: Grab audio at up to 320 kbps MP3 or pure lossless FLAC. Prefer video? Extract pristine 4K and 1080p clips with full audio synchronization.
- **Album Artwork Included**: Every downloaded song comes with high-resolution cover art, artist tags, and album metadata embedded right into the file.
- **Sing Along with Lyrics**: Automatically fetch synchronized, karaoke-style lyrics and plain-text song words for your favorite tracks.

## Highlights

### Vintage Analog Aesthetics
Experience the golden era of hi-fi cassette decks right on your desktop. Enjoy authentic cassette labels, moving tape ribbon mechanics, stereo peak indicators, and classic transport buttons.

### Smart Collection Organization
Whether you listen to Bollywood hits, Punjabi anthems, K-Pop, Latin beats, Japanese city pop, or Western classics, OSMIIC automatically detects languages and regional artists to place every track into its proper collection folder.

### Full Playlist and Album Support
Paste a playlist or channel URL to preview every track with thumbnails and durations. Select exactly what you want or download the entire batch with one click.

### Instant Clipboard Detection
Copy any media link in your browser, and OSMIIC immediately offers to add it to your queue without manual copy-pasting.

### Access Member and Restricted Content
Import cookies from your preferred browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge, Opera, and more) with one click to download subscriber-only and restricted content seamlessly.

### Subtitle Extraction
Grab subtitles and closed captions in your preferred languages in standard SRT or VTT formats.

### Smart Duplicate Protection
Never accidentally download the same song twice. Choose to skip existing tracks, replace older versions, or auto-rename with clean numbering.

### Desktop Notifications and System Tray
Queue your downloads, minimize OSMIIC to your system tray, and get a gentle notification when your tape is ready.

## Getting Started

### 1. Download OSMIIC
Grab the latest release for your desktop:
- **Linux**: AppImage or Debian package (.deb)
- **Windows**: Setup installer (.exe) or Windows Installer (.msi)

### 2. Paste or Copy a Link
Copy any music or video URL from YouTube or supported platforms. OSMIIC detects the link on your clipboard or lets you paste it into the deck.

### 3. Hit Record
Choose your format (Audio or Video), pick your quality preference, and press the Record button. OSMIIC handles downloading, tagging, and organizing automatically.
